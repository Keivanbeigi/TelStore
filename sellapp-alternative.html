Sell App alternative — or a shop bot that lives in Telegram

Comparing hosted digital storefronts with a self-hosted bot that sells directly in chat.

What Sell App gets right

Sell App is a well-designed, developer-friendly hosted storefront for selling digital downloads. It offers a polished checkout experience, solid file hosting infrastructure, integrated fraud prevention, and responsive customer-facing product pages out of the box.

The onboarding experience is smooth: you create an account, upload your digital deliverables, connect a payment gateway, and immediately receive a professional web link to distribute to buyers. For digital creators who want a dedicated web store without touching backend configuration or hosting management, Sell App delivers exactly what it promises.

The platform handles customer email notifications, secure file delivery links, and payment confirmations smoothly. If you need a clean browser-based web store and want no operational responsibilities, it is a sound and reliable tool.

The difference that matters: where the buyer already is

The distinction between a hosted storefront and a Telegram sales bot is not about quality—it is about where your buyers spend their attention. A hosted storefront directs customers to an external web page. The customer must click a link, wait for an external browser tab to open, navigate a web checkout form, complete payment, and wait for an email link or a download page to load before returning to their messaging app.

A Telegram shop bot operates entirely inside the conversation your buyer is already having. When someone is in your channel or chat group, browsing products, selecting payment, and receiving the delivered file or VIP invite link happens seamlessly in that same chat window. There is no redirect, no browser tab to switch between, and no external registration required.

The second difference is ownership. With a hosted storefront, you rent your checkout pipeline indefinitely and pay a percentage of every sale to third parties. With a self-hosted bot like TelStore, your shop runs on your own server, governed by 12 Python modules that you can inspect, modify, and host on your own terms for a single $29 fee. Getting up and running takes about 10 minutes on a fresh Ubuntu VPS:

unzip TelStore-Bot.zip -d /opt
cd /opt/telstore
cp scripts/.env.example scripts/.env      # add your bot token and wallet
sudo bash scripts/install_telstore.sh --create-service
sudo systemctl start telstore-bot

When a hosted storefront is the better answer

TelStore is intentionally not the right choice for every merchant. If you do not already run a Linux VPS and do not want to manage one, setting up a self-hosted bot creates unnecessary overhead. A hosted storefront eliminates server patching, systemd management, and script execution entirely.

Similarly, if your primary audience finds you through web search, social feeds outside Telegram, or traditional desktop websites, sending those customers into a Telegram bot creates friction rather than reducing it. A standard browser checkout page is the expected, natural shopping interface for web-first audiences.

If you prefer a managed web dashboard over interacting with a bot inside Telegram chat, and you do not want to inspect or modify Python source code, choose a hosted storefront. TelStore is designed specifically for sellers whose active community lives in Telegram and who want complete custody over their customer data, files, and transaction flow.

Questions people ask before switching

Do I need a server?

Yes. Any Ubuntu 22.04 or 24.04 VPS. The bot idles at roughly 20 MB of RAM.

Do I need to know Python?

No, to run it. The install is one script. Yes, if you want to change behaviour — that is the point of owning the source.

How does delivery work inside Telegram?

The moment payment confirms, the bot automatically sends the file directly into the buyer's Telegram chat or generates a single-use invite link to your private VIP channel.

Can I still take card payments?

Yes, through NOWPayments, which handles cards as well as crypto.
