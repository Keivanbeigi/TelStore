FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a merchant account?

No. Payments go straight to your wallet, or through NOWPayments if you want card support.

Which coins?

Any EVM token on BSC (USDT, BNB), plus USDT/TRON. NOWPayments adds BTC, ETH and more.

Can I sell to buyers without crypto?

Yes, through NOWPayments, which accepts cards. CoinGate is also supported once your account passes KYC.

What if a buyer underpays?

The bot does not deliver. It flags the order and asks the admin to approve or reject it in one tap.

What do I need to run it?

A small Linux VPS (1 vCPU / 2 GB is enough) and a Telegram bot token from BotFather.

Is it a subscription?

No. One payment, $29, source code yours.
