## Features

- **Multi-product shop** — each product has its own price, duration, and category (channel access, digital downloads, courses, tools, eBooks…)
- **VIP channel auto-membership** — after payment the buyer is automatically invited to your private channel
- **Instant digital delivery** — send links / files / text to the buyer the moment payment confirms
- **Crypto payments, no bank card** — manual wallet (BSC / Ethereum / Polygon) **plus** optional NOWPayments and CoinGate card/crypto checkout
- **Owner admin panel** — add / remove / list products from Telegram, step-by-step wizard, broadcast to subscribers, view stats, edit settings
- **One-time products** — a product (like the full source) can be configured to disappear from the shop after its single sale
- **Per-sale notifications** — the owner gets a new-sale alert with the buyer's TXID
- **Clean, documented, English code** — `config.py` (settings), `lang.py` (all text), `bot.py` (logic) are separated so any human can extend it
